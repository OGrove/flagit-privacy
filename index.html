Flag It — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Flag It is a personal reminder tool that lets you flag screenshots, text, and links so you can review them later. This policy explains how Flag It handles your information. The short version: everything stays on your device. Flag It does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data.

Information we collect

None. Flag It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers. The screenshots, text, links, notes, and any markup you save are stored only in the app's private storage on your own phone. This information is never sent to us or to any third party.

How your content is used

The content you flag is shown back to you inside the app and used to produce your daily reminders. Text inside your screenshots is read using on-device text recognition so you can search it — this processing happens entirely on your phone, and the extracted text is stored locally alongside the screenshot.

Permissions

  • Notifications — to show the daily reminders you schedule.
  • Receiving shared images, text, and links — so you can send content into Flag It from another app's Share menu.
  • Deleting screenshots from your device (Android) — only when you choose to, and only after the system's own confirmation dialog.
  • Running after restart (Android) — so your scheduled reminders survive a phone restart.

Data sharing

Flag It does not share your data with anyone. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party services embedded in the app.

Data retention and deletion

Your flags remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. You can delete any flag at any time from within the app. Uninstalling Flag It removes all of its stored data from your device.

Children

Flag It is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13.
